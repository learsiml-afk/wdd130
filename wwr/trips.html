Choose Your Adventure

Los Dientes del Diablo

Class IV · Full Day

Our signature trip takes you through the legendary "Devil's Teeth" – a series of powerful Class IV rapids carved through ancient volcanic rock. Expert guides lead small groups of up to 8 through 6 hours of pure adrenaline. Minimum age 16. All safety gear provided.

  • Duration: 6 hours
  • Difficulty: Class IV
  • Min. Age: 16
  • Group Size: 4–8

Half Day Adventure

Class II–III · Half Day

Perfect for families and first-timers. This 3-hour scenic float combines mild rapids with stunning river canyon views. You'll learn basic paddle techniques from our guides while soaking in the natural beauty of the Black River. Suitable for ages 8 and up.

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Difficulty: Class II–III
  • Min. Age: 8
  • Group Size: 4–12

Salmon River Expedition

Class III–IV · 2 Days

Spend two unforgettable days running the Salmon River's best stretches. Camping overnight along the riverbank under the stars, this expedition is the ultimate escape. Meals, camping gear, and all rafting equipment are included. Limited availability – book early!

  • Duration: 2 days / 1 night
  • Difficulty: Class III–IV
  • Min. Age: 14
  • Group Size: 4–10
